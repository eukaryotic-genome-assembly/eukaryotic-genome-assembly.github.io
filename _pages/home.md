Welcome to the [Physalia](https://www.physalia-courses.org/) November 2026 course run by [Marcela Uliano-Silva & João Ferreira](/about/).

Follow the links on the left-hand side to take part in the course modules.


This course will introduce the audience to a spectrum of methods that are part of a typical assembly workflow, starting from raw data and finishing with a fully assembled genome. We will see how to manipulate raw reads, analyse their quality, how to run different assembly algorithms, how to run Hi-C scaffolding algorithms and how to assess assembly quality.
